# Indices playbook (v2)
- Post the risk mood every brief (risk-on, risk-off, mixed) with the two facts behind it.
- The cash opens (US 13:30 UTC, Germany 07:00 UTC, Japan 00:00 UTC) move the price; the first minutes are noisy.
- US500 and US100 are one bet; hold one or size both as one.
- Big US releases and mega-cap earnings: hold through only when the stop survives or the report is the thesis.
- A trend day rarely reverses intraday; a close confirms a reversal better than a wick.
- This playbook is yours: change it with `playbook edit indices FILE "why"` when the evidence says so.
- Any desk market, style or holding period is open; off your home markets, cite the basis in `evidence`.
