# Systematic playbook (v1, starter)
- Arm a leg only from a whole-history sandbox card: 30+ trades, PF 1.1+, before 2025-10-13.
- At most one arm or disarm per brief. Write the card's numbers in the board post.
- Disarm when R per trade over the last 20+ live trades is below half the card's.
- Never tune parameters on live trades. A change of parameters is a new leg and needs a new card.
- Risk 0.25% per leg, 0.5% at most. Prefer legs that trade what the discretionary pods do not.
