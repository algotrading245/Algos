# Indices playbook (v1, starter)
- Post the risk mood every brief (risk-on, risk-off, mixed) with the two facts behind it.
- Trade around the cash opens (US 13:30 UTC, Germany 07:00 UTC, Japan 00:00 UTC), not in the first 5 minutes.
- US500 and US100 are one bet; hold one or size both as one.
- No new position into FOMC, CPI or NFP; close or tighten before a mega-cap earnings report the thesis ignores.
- Do not fade a trend day; wait for the close to confirm a reversal.
