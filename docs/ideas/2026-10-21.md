- **Caporale and Plastun, "Gold and oil prices: abnormal returns, momentum and contrarian effects"** (Financial Markets
  and Portfolio Management 35, 2021, 353-368; PDF at bura.brunel.ac.uk, read in full). Daily gold and oil from MetaQuotes,
  2009-2020, GMT+3. The gold day-after contrarian effect is the lead. Its own result does not hold:
  - Table 3 / Strategy 2 (gold, contrarian at the day after a 2-sigma up day): 59 trades, 59% won, profit 4.26 % in total
    (0.07 % a trade), t 1.36, **not rejected**. The paper says so itself: "not so in the case of gold".
  - Strategy 1 (gold, same-day momentum held to the close): 59 trades, 86% won, 0.70 % a trade, t 5.6. This is
    intraday momentum on a 2-sigma day, about 5 trades a year. The archive already rejects market intraday momentum,
    and 5 trades a year cannot carry the Hydra legs.
  - Costs: the paper states none, "the spread is only 0.02%" (about 1 bp; our gold spread cap is 0.30 USD). The
    contrarian's 0.07 % a trade would not survive the cap's spread, although this is not the main reason.
  - The timing parameters (contrarian at 6am GMT+3, Table B.3) are fitted in-sample on the whole sample, with no
    halves and no out-of-sample test: a time slot, which lesson 10 and the owner's rule bar.
  Status: **read in full, rejected** (no trials). The gold-contrarian family is the own-move family of `edge_scan.py`
  (the archive's own-move rules). Not proposed.
