## Why entries did not pair

Tester only (4):
- midday 10-19: the demo was attached at 14:54, after the entry time.
- breakout 10-19: the tester entered at 10:56 server time, before the demo was attached. The demo's late entry was logged as a "10009 failed".
- noise 10-19 17:30 local: the demo filled, but the build from before the fix logged it as "10009 failed".
- Tokyo 10-20 02:55 local: the demo filled 0.63 at 158.558, but the same old build logged it as "10009 failed".

Demo only (2), both on the breakout leg:
- 10-20 09:40, sold 0.02 at 158.106: the tester skipped it with "USD/JPY M1 history since the start is incomplete (0 of 37 bars)". The tester has a gap in its data.
- 10-23 09:06, bought 0.02 at 157.123: the tester skipped it with "balance too small for the minimum lot of USDJPY". The tester's balance had drifted below the demo's, and the leg sizes at the minimum lot.

So every unpaired entry comes from attach timing, the old logging bug, or a borderline in the tester's data or sizing. None comes from different signal logic.

## Findings

1. **Signals match.** Every paired entry has the same price and time on both runs.
2. **Breakout leg sits at the minimum lot at 10k EUR.** It trades 0.02 USDJPY, and a small balance difference flips it between a trade and "balance too small". That explains the 1.50 lot ratio and one of the demo-only entries.
3. **The tester's USDJPY M1 history has a gap on 10-20.** The tester had 0 of 37 bars, so it skipped a trade the demo took.
4. **Midday lot outlier on 10-20:** the demo traded 0.44 and the tester 0.28 at the same model weight of 1.23. The demo's Tokyo position closed at 04:25 local, just after the 04:14 reload. In the tester, Tokyo (sold 0.61 at 03:55 server) was probably still open at midday, so the open-risk cap shrank the midday lot. This is not fully confirmed: no tester close line for Tokyo was found between 03:00 and 14:00 server time. On the other days the midday ratio is about 1.05, in line with the balance difference.
5. **10009 lines were logged by the old build.** All three (10-19 14:54 breakout, 10-19 17:30 noise, 10-20 02:55 Tokyo) come before the 10-20 04:14 reload, and the 10009 fix landed in 1d49ede. There are none after the reload.
6. **Duplicate closes:** a second close was sent to positions that had already closed, giving 10013 on USDJPY at 10-19 17:00 and 10036 on EURUSD at 10-23 14:00. Fixed in AurumFunded 1.11 / AurumHydra 1.40 (dd9046b): no second close goes out while one sent in the last 10 s is still in flight, and 10036 counts as done. Terminal 16 picks this up on its next restart or re-attach.
