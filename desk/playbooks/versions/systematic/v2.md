# Systematic playbook (v2)
- Arm from a whole-history sandbox card before 2025-10-13 (the desk requires 30+ trades, PF 1.1+).
- Write the card's numbers in the board post when you arm or disarm.
- Disarm when live R per trade over 20+ trades is well below the card's, or the market changed.
- Never tune parameters on live trades. A change of parameters is a new leg and needs a new card.
- Size by evidence: 0.25% is a normal leg. Prefer legs that trade what the discretionary pods do not.
- Judgment tickets on any market are open to you like any pod.
- This playbook is yours: change it with `playbook edit systematic FILE "why"` when the evidence says so.
- Any desk market, style or holding period is open; off your home markets, cite the basis in `evidence`.
