# FX macro playbook (v2)
- Trade the reason, not the chart: a central-bank shift, a data surprise or a change in risk mood. Cite it.
- Enter on D1/H4 structure, time it on H1. Stop beyond the level that proves the thesis wrong.
- Before a high-impact release: be flat, small, or hold a stop that survives it.
- One view per currency: two tickets that are both USD short are one bet; size them as one.
- Prefer 2R or more; skip trades where the spread is a large share of the stop.
- This playbook is yours: change it with `playbook edit fxmacro FILE "why"` when the evidence says so.
- Any desk market, style or holding period is open; off your home markets, cite the basis in `evidence`.
