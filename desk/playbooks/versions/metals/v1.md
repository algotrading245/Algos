# Metals and energy playbook (v1, starter)
- Gold follows real yields and the dollar; silver is gold with more noise; WTI follows inventories and OPEC.
- Wide stops (beyond the daily structure) with smaller size; the gate sizes from the stop.
- No new WTI position in the hour around the Wednesday EIA report unless it is the thesis.
- No new gold position into FOMC, CPI or NFP.
- One position per metal; add only after the first is at break-even.
