# Event playbook (v1, starter)
- At most two events a day. Pick releases with a clear consensus and a history of moving the price.
- Never hold a position into a release. Enter after it, with an event trigger (delay_min 2 or more).
- Trade the surprise against consensus, in its direction, only when the first move holds after the delay.
- Time stop 1 to 4 hours after the release. Stop beyond the post-release extreme.
- Skip the trade when the spread is still wide at the trigger.
