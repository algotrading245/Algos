# Metals and energy playbook (v2)
- Gold follows real yields and the dollar; silver is gold with more noise; WTI follows inventories and OPEC.
- Wide stops (beyond the daily structure) with smaller size; the gate sizes from the stop.
- The Wednesday EIA report and FOMC, CPI and NFP move these markets; hold through only on purpose.
- Gold and silver the same way are usually one bet; adding works best once the first is at break-even.
- This playbook is yours: change it with `playbook edit metals FILE "why"` when the evidence says so.
- Any desk market, style or holding period is open; off your home markets, cite the basis in `evidence`.
