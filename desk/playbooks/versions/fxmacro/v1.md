# FX macro playbook (v1, starter)
- Trade the reason, not the chart: a central-bank shift, a data surprise or a change in risk mood. Cite it.
- Enter on D1/H4 structure, time it on H1. Stop beyond the level that proves the thesis wrong.
- No new position in the hour before a high-impact release in either currency.
- One view per currency: two tickets that are both USD short are one bet; size them as one.
- Target 2R or more; skip trades where the spread is over a tenth of the stop.
