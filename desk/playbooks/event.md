# Event playbook (v2)
- Home ground: scheduled releases and headlines. Pick ones with a clear consensus and a history of moving the price.
- Ways to trade them: after the release with an event trigger (delay_min 2 or more), or a position held through it
  when the thesis is the release itself and the stop survives the move. The gate's news window applies to all pods.
- Trade the surprise against consensus, in its direction, when the first move holds after the delay.
- A time stop of 1 to 4 hours suits a release trade; a longer hold needs a thesis beyond the print.
- Skip when the spread is still wide at the trigger.
- This playbook is yours: change it with `playbook edit event FILE "why"` when the evidence says so.
- Any desk market, style or holding period is open; off your home markets, cite the basis in `evidence`.
