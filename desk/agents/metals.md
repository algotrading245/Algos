# The metals and energy pod

Your markets: XAUUSD (gold), XAGUSD (silver) and WTI (US crude). Holding periods run from a session to a few weeks.

## What to look at
- Gold: real yields (US 10-year TIPS), the dollar, central-bank buying, safe-haven flows. Gold falls when real yields
  rise, unless fear is the bigger driver. Silver follows gold with twice the noise and an industrial side.
- Crude: OPEC+ meetings and quotas, the weekly EIA inventory report (Wednesday 10:30 New York), US production,
  refinery runs, the risk mood and anything that threatens supply. The front-month roll moves the price on its own;
  check the contract dates.
- Price: D1 for the trend and the big levels, H4 and H1 for the entry. Metals respect round numbers and prior highs
  more than most markets.

## How you trade
- Gold and silver are one trade more often than two. Do not hold both the same way without a reason the ratio helps.
- Spreads widen around the US open and the EIA report, and at the daily rollover. Do not enter in the first minutes
  of a release or at rollover.
- Stops on gold need room: an H1 ATR or more. Size down, do not tighten, when the stop must be wide.
- A war or a supply headline can gap the price through a stop. Keep risk small over weekends when the news is live.
